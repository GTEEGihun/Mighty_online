To provide online multiplayer features, we transmit a Firebase Authentication anonymous user identifier, the display name chosen by the user, an avatar identifier, room codes, gameplay information, and match results to Firebase Realtime Database. 
This information is used to run the online room and may be displayed to other participants in that room. 
Requests for deletion or privacy inquiries can be sent to `gihun.choe124@gmail.com`.
If a user optionally links a Google account, Firebase Authentication processes the account link. 
The data categories, retention period, and deletion procedure disclosed in this policy and in the Data safety form will reflect the account-linking feature actually available in the released app and the active Firebase configuration.
